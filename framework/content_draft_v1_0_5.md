### Section: The Problem (sets up why you exist)

**Label:** Why catalog problems compound

**Headline:**
Mismatched products. Duplicate listings. Suppressed listings.
Most catalog problems start with process.

**Body:**
A product goes live on three channels with three different titles. A UPC gets reused across variants and now you have duplicates you can't untangle. A listing gets suppressed for a content issue nobody catches for weeks.

These aren't tool problems — they're process problems. The workflow that creates, updates, and syncs product data across channels wasn't designed for the scale or complexity it's now handling. And the symptoms keep compounding until someone with deep catalog experience steps in and traces the problem back to its source.

---

### Section: Services (6 cards)

**Label:** What I do

**Headline:**
Get your products live, listed, found, and purchasable.

**Card 1: Account Health Check**
A structured review of your marketplace accounts — identifying suppressed listings, policy flags, content gaps, and performance issues before they compound. You get a clear picture of where things stand and what needs attention first.

**Card 2: Data Quality Review**
A diagnostic of your product data across channels — mismatched attributes, duplicate listings, encoding issues, missing content, and UPC conflicts. Prioritized by impact so you know where to focus.

**Card 3: Catalog Integration & Channel Setup**
Getting your product data from wherever it lives to wherever it needs to go — Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Target, and beyond — with the right field mapping, category taxonomy, and content standards for each channel.

**Card 4: Listing Optimization**
Fixing the content, structure, and classification issues that cause products to underperform or disappear. Titles, attributes, images, and channel-specific requirements — aligned to what each platform actually rewards.

**Card 5: Search & Findability**
Getting a product listed is not the same as getting it found. Consumer search behavior varies by channel — and AI-powered discovery on Amazon, Walmart, and Google is changing what surfaces and what doesn't. Understanding how buyers actually search, and structuring product content accordingly, is what separates a visible product from an invisible one.

**Card 6: GTM Strategy for Catalog Products**
Channel activation planning and launch sequencing for teams bringing new products or platforms to market across complex multi-channel environments.
