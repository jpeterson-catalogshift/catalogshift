## CatalogShift

### Purpose of This Document

This document provides context for all work related to **CatalogShift**. It is intended to be used with AI systems or collaborators so that work produced for CatalogShift reflects consistent positioning, voice, and direction.

CatalogShift is currently a **domain and emerging platform**, not a fully developed product or company. The immediate goal is to establish a body of thinking and practical frameworks around enterprise AI usage, data systems, and operational context.

This document defines:
- What CatalogShift is
- What problems it explores
- The intended audience
- The voice and tone of the content
- How future work should be structured

---

### 1. Core Concept

CatalogShift explores how **enterprise systems thinking applies to modern AI systems.**

Many current discussions about AI focus on: prompts, productivity hacks, coding assistants, and individual usage.

CatalogShift focuses on a different layer: **how AI actually operates inside real organizations.**

This includes:
- Context management
- Operational data systems
- Enterprise workflows
- Integration architecture
- Decision context
- Data quality realities

> AI systems perform best when they are embedded in structured operational context, not when they are treated as standalone tools.

---

### 2. Origin of the Name

The name **CatalogShift** comes from Jeremy Peterson's background in large-scale commerce systems. For nearly two decades, Jeremy worked with product catalogs, marketplace integrations, enterprise data pipelines, and catalog quality problems at scale.

The "shift" refers to a broader transition:

```
from catalog-driven systems
to context-driven AI systems
```

CatalogShift represents the evolution of data systems thinking into the AI era.

### 4. The Problems CatalogShift Explores

#### Context Loss
AI systems operate without memory of organizational history unless that context is deliberately provided. This leads to repeated explanations, inconsistent outputs, and poor alignment with real objectives.

#### Data Quality Reality
Most AI demonstrations assume clean data. Real enterprise environments contain incomplete records, inconsistent identifiers, legacy data formats, missing content, and conflicting sources. Understanding this reality is critical to building useful AI systems.

#### Operational Integration
The key question is not *what can AI generate?* The real question is *what happens to the output once it enters the operational system?*

Examples include:
- Responses entering CRM workflows
- AI output feeding data pipelines
- Analysis driving operational decisions

AI must be integrated into the systems where work actually happens.

---

### 5. Core Themes

#### Context Architecture
Designing structured context environments that allow AI systems to operate effectively.

#### Enterprise Discovery Thinking
Applying traditional consulting discovery frameworks to AI usage. Example structure:
```
WHO / WHAT / WHERE / WHEN / HOW / WHY
```
This approach forms the foundation of the **Context Before Prompting** framework.

#### Data Systems Thinking
Understanding how data flows through systems, including ingestion, transformation, storage, interpretation, and downstream use.

#### Operational AI
Moving AI usage from experimental interaction to repeatable operational workflows.

### 7. Voice and Tone

CatalogShift content should reflect the voice of a practitioner with extensive real-world experience.

**Practical** — Examples should be grounded in real operational scenarios. Avoid abstract theory unless it directly connects to practice.

**Reflective** — The tone should feel observational rather than declarative:
```
Here is something I noticed.
Here is what it revealed.
Here is how it connects to AI systems.
```

**Calm and Analytical** — Avoid hype language. CatalogShift should sound like systems thinking, architecture discussion, and operational analysis — not AI marketing or startup evangelism.

## Context Document 2

---

### Overview

CatalogShift is an early-stage project exploring how artificial intelligence can improve product catalog quality and product data workflows in e-commerce systems.

The project currently exists as a **domain name, concept framework, and research effort** focused on understanding how AI can help organizations transform unstructured product information into usable catalog data.

CatalogShift may evolve into one or more of the following:
- An **AI-powered software product**
- An **AI consulting practice**
- A **hybrid model combining consulting and software tools**

---

### Vision

CatalogShift exists to help organizations turn messy, incomplete, and fragmented product information into structured catalog data that can power modern commerce systems.

---

### Problem Space

Most product data in commerce organizations is fragmented across PDFs, packaging labels, marketing documents, instruction manuals, spreadsheets, and internal systems.

As a result, product catalogs are often incomplete, inconsistent, and difficult to scale across channels such as Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Shopify, and retail partner systems.

AI creates an opportunity to **extract, interpret, and normalize this information automatically**.

---

### Initial Focus Areas

**1. Product Content Extraction** — Use AI to extract structured product information from documents, images, videos, packaging, and marketing materials.

**2. Catalog Data Structuring** — Transform extracted information into structured fields required for commerce systems (titles, attributes, bullet points, specifications, compatibility information).

**3. Channel-Ready Product Data** — Prepare product content for marketplace and commerce platforms (Amazon listing format, Walmart item setup, Shopify product catalog, retailer data feeds).

---

### Potential Product Direction

A system that allows users to upload product videos, photos, instruction manuals, and specification sheets — then generates structured product content, attributes, listing descriptions, and marketplace-ready product data.

---

### Consulting Direction

CatalogShift may also operate as a consulting service focused on:
- AI adoption in commerce
- Product catalog architecture
- Data quality improvement
- Marketplace data strategy
- AI-assisted catalog workflows

---

### Current Status

CatalogShift is currently in **concept development and research phase**.

Current activities include:
- AI capability exploration
- Framework development
- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Architecture planning
- Platform experimentation (AWS, Claude, AI tooling)

---

### Guiding Principle

**AI is only as good as the context and data it receives.**

CatalogShift focuses on building the **systems, context, and workflows** that allow AI to work effectively with real-world product data.
