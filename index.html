Why catalog problems compound

Mismatched products. Duplicate listings. Suppressed listings.

These aren't tool problems — they're process problems. The workflow that creates, updates, and syncs product data across channels wasn't designed for the scale or complexity it's now handling. And the symptoms keep compounding until someone with deep catalog experience steps in and traces the problem back to its source.

  • ⚠️

    A product goes live on three channels with three different titles, attributes, or prices — and nobody catches it until a customer complains.
  • 🔁
    Duplicate listings
    A UPC gets reused across variants and now you have duplicates you can't untangle — splitting reviews, diluting rank, and confusing buyers.
  • 🚫
    Suppressed listings
    A listing gets suppressed for a content issue nobody catches for weeks. Revenue silently disappears while the product sits invisible on the shelf.

Search & Findability

Getting a product listed is not the same as getting it found. Consumer search behavior varies by channel — and AI-powered discovery on Amazon, Walmart, and Google is changing what surfaces and what doesn't. Understanding how buyers actually search, and structuring product content accordingly, is what separates a visible product from an invisible one.
