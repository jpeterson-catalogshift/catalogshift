About

28 years of building things
that work when nobody's watching.

I'm Jeremy Peterson — a catalog and integration specialist with deep experience helping ecommerce brands, manufacturers, and retailers get their product data right across every channel they sell on.

CatalogShift is built around a simple idea: most catalog problems are solvable. Suppressed listings, duplicate products, mismatched data, channels that won't sync — these aren't permanent conditions. They're process gaps, and process gaps can be closed.

I work with teams that need someone who can diagnose the real problem, build the right solution, and get products live, listed, and performing.
